# Risk Score

The Risk Score evaluates the potential danger a Pull Request introduces. 
**IMPORTANT:** Risk != 100 - Quality.

## Verified Weights
- Security: 40%
- Change Surface: 25%
- Sensitive Path: 20%
- Complexity: 15%

## Levels
- LOW (0-30)
- MEDIUM (31-60)
- HIGH (61-85)
- CRITICAL (86-100)
